Orange Medical Care — SMS Privacy Policy
Effective date: November 6, 2026

Orange Medical Care (“Orange Medical Care,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) provides customer-care text messages to patients who choose to receive them. Messages may include appointment reminders, scheduling updates, and other service-related notifications.

This SMS Privacy Policy explains how we handle information connected with those messages. It supplements—not replaces—our Notice of Privacy Practices and other privacy notices.

Information we collect
Depending on your interaction with us, we may collect your mobile number, your SMS consent and opt-out status, message and delivery records, and limited information needed to send or respond to a service-related text. Please do not send diagnoses, test results, treatment details, or other sensitive medical information by text.

How we use information
We use this information to send and manage the messages you requested, respond to SMS-related questions, maintain consent and opt-out records, and meet legal or regulatory requirements. Our SMS program is for customer-care messages, not promotional or marketing messages.

Sharing and disclosure
We do not sell or rent mobile numbers. We do not share mobile numbers or SMS consent information with affiliates or third parties for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

We may provide the minimum necessary information to service providers that help us deliver or support requested text messages. They may use it only to provide those services for us. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect rights and safety. SMS opt-in data and consent will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for their own purposes.

Consumer Contact Information
If you have questions about our SMS communications or privacy practices, please contact the message sender using the contact information provided on our website.

SMS Data Transfers
Your data will not be transferred to external organizations.

SMS Opt-Out and Help

To stop receiving SMS messages, reply STOP at any time.
For assistance, reply HELP.

Privacy and security
We use reasonable safeguards for information handled through our SMS program. Standard text messages may not be encrypted end-to-end and could be seen by someone with access to your phone or notifications. We aim to keep messages limited to administrative and service-related information, but cannot guarantee the privacy or delivery of a text message.

If you prefer not to receive texts, or do not want appointment information sent by SMS, contact us using the details below. You may also opt out as described in our SMS Terms and Conditions.

Retention
We retain SMS-related information as reasonably necessary to operate the program, document consent and opt-out requests, and comply with applicable recordkeeping requirements.

Children and guardians
A parent or legal guardian should provide consent for SMS messages sent to or about a minor patient when appropriate. A guardian may opt out at any time.
